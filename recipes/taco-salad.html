Taco Salad

About the Dish

Taco Salad is an easy and delicious meal that is great for snacking on by yourself or making for large groups.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Ground Beef
  • 1 Packet Taco Seasoning
  • 1 Head Romaine Lettuce
  • 3 Roma Tomatoes
  • 1 Cup Shredded Cheddar Cheese
  • 1 Bag Fritos
  • 2 Cups Sour Cream

Steps

  1. Put Ground Beef and Taco Seasoning in a pan and cook until meat is brown.
  2. Chop Lettuce and Tomatoes.
  3. Combine all ingredients together and mix.
