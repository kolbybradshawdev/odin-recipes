Lomo Saltado

About the Dish

Lomo Saltado is a classic Peruvian dish. The name of the dish literally translates to Jumping Loin, although the actual meaning of jumping in this case is stir-fried.

It is often served with rice, thick cut fries, and stir-fried veggies, along with a classic Peruvian soda such as Coke or the Peruvian exclusive Inca Cola.


Ingredients

  • 1 lb Sirloin Beef
  • 1 Red Onion
  • 4 Roma Tomatoes
  • 4 Cloves of Garlic
  • 1 Aji Amarillo Pepper
  • 5 tbsp Soy Sauce
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Red Wine Vinegar
  • French Fries
  • Rice

Steps

  1. Cut the Beef to bite sized pieces.
  2. Combine Soy Sauce, Red Wine Vinegar, and Beef in a bowl.
  3. Pour contents into a pan and cook on medium heat, stirring ocassionaly.
  4. After 5-10 minutes (depending on how cooked you want the veggies), add the chopped Onion,Tomatoes, Garlic, and Pepper.
  5. Continusously stir until the meat is cooked and the veggies are to your liking.
  6. Serve with rice and french fries.
