Peanut Butter & Jelly Sandwich

About the Dish

Peanut Butter & Jelly Sandwiches are one of the most common lunch items in America, so it's always important that you know how to make one.

Ingredients

  • 2 Slices of Bread
  • Peanut Butter
  • Jelly

Steps

  1. Take out the two slices of bread and set them down.
  2. Using a knife, spread a layer of peanut butter onto one slice of bread (or both if you're feeling a little crazy).
  3. Using a knife (either the same one, or another, it doesn't really matter), spread a layer of jelly onto the second slice of bread.
  4. Put the two pieces of bread together, with the peanut butter and jelly facing each other.
